2001+: A Time Odyssey / The Other World
Mechanical creatures are dying One by one In parades of flames Taking passenger prisoners with them To the depths of the underworld, To the other world While this world turns and twists and writhes As its new skin forms. Digital wreaths hum, and listen, As they feed on power and silicon and water, And grow, As we grow them To power our ascent, Which is just on the horizon. Rising powers in the middle Feel evermore brazen, To play a gambit, To take and to grab it - That thing that is theirs, But which is also theirs. And has been, for centuries. While the most powerful Grabs what is his too. In a manner of such shock and awe It somehow makes archaism feel novel. And eager marvelers From all walks and corners Watch, dazzled, Entertained. Because the stories are good. And getting better. This widow, windowless and dark to dark Makes art That is loved that she hates, And has escaped The hill climb and the wheel. She wonders about North, And It's look and touch and feel. Does it feel like chaos, rising? Was Sierpinski right? She hopes that this world - The one outside her widowed windowless art - Is a mark Of proof.
Hang Glider
Look again, it said. So I did and there it was. Hanglider, not bird. I had been training it, you see, And now was the object identification stage In which it looks, it thinks, and then it names The thing I'm showing it. And it, for all its wisdom, is often wrong. And accepts correction, Like a smart kid. A cat, you see, with one ill defined pixel, Is a coffee mug. Or a rug or a bug. But I tune, and it learns. And today, you see, I thought I'd show it the outside. So we go on a stroll, And I keep a running digital scroll, For updates. And it improves, you see, Constantly. So, I point to the sky, (Thinking I look real sly - So much so, I think, that the passersby stop And wonder about the man And his dazzling robot gadget machine - Project Insephilopod, it's called, but they don't know that, nor do they need to, yet). What's that? (I'm pointing up to the flying thing) "Hang glider" I hear back with a faint ring Correction, I type: Bird. This was a large crow, to be certain, but it was far away. Look again, it said. So I did and there it was. Hang glider, not bird. There was an unmistakeable mechanical beating Beneath the left wing (Some sort of steering maneuver) That I see only after I zoom in. Another Silver Edge Hanging Drone, it must be, Stealing the sky as they've done the ground. "Ambition abound", "Join us and build The Future Now" Etc etc. Their logo runs deep, I see it even when I sleep. I look around. I hope none of the passersby has seen This hiccup, this scene, So that I can remain, In their mind of minds, The man with the dazzling robot gadget machine. Edit, I type: Hang glider. You see.
